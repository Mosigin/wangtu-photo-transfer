# 无文字应用图标

`logo.png` 为1024×1024不透明PNG。相同图像用于iOS AppIcon，外框由系统遮罩处理。以相框、圆形日光和向右上流动的箭头表达媒体直传；无文字、字母或平台商标。

使用内置 imagegen 生成，随后仅做标准尺寸准备。提示词：

> Use case: logo-brand. Create a finished 1024x1024 square iOS app icon for an independent photo-and-video transfer utility. Absolutely no text, letters, initials, words, numerals or watermarks. A distinctive simple centered symbol combines a rounded photo frame with a small circular sun and a flowing upward/right transfer arrow emerging naturally from the frame, suggesting media moving directly from a computer into a phone. Strong recognisable silhouette at small size, polished restrained geometric design, subtle depth, balanced negative space. Rich deep midnight-indigo full-bleed opaque square background and a luminous coral-to-apricot main symbol with a small warm-ivory highlight. Do not depict an actual phone or laptop, do not use any Apple, iCloud, GitHub or other brand logos. One coherent logo only, not a sheet of variants, no mockup, no outer border, no extra canvas margin, no pre-rounded outer corners: the full square background must reach all four edges. App-store-ready 1024px square composition.
